## Selected Publications

1. **Context-Aligned Contrastive Regression for Multilingual Lexical Complexity Prediction** - First Author, ACL-BEA 2026 Shared Task ([arXiv:2605.08950](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08950)). Proposed a ridge-regression ensemble with cross-view context and ordinal soft contrastive learning for vocabulary difficulty prediction.

2. **ITLC at SemEval-2026 Task 11: Normalization and Deterministic Parsing for Formal Reasoning in LLMs** - First Author, ACL-SemEval-2026 ([arXiv:2603.02676](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.02676)). Top-5 across all subtasks on multilingual syllogistic reasoning with deterministic parsing.

3. **Aligning Implied Statements for Implicit Hate Speech Generalizability** - First Author, under peer review at ACL Rolling Review ([OpenReview](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=pgwGGyy77k)). Improved out-of-domain F1 by 4-8% across three benchmarks using triplet training with context-bounded semi-hard negatives.

4. **LLM-Based Conversational PHQ-Aligned Screening (CPAS)** - Person in Charge (ML), under review at IEEE Access and ACM TiiS. Built a PHQ-9 aligned screening workflow combining dialogue, questionnaire, and facial affect analysis.

5. **Automated Detection of Semantic and Pragmatic Quality Issues in User Stories** - Research Assistant, presented at ICODSE 2025. Extended AQUSA with LLM-assisted pragmatic and semantic checks for user stories.

6. **On-Edge Device Optimization for Audio Classification** - First Author, published at IEEE Xplore. Deployed an MFCC-based classifier on Arduino Nano 33 BLE with 95.2% accuracy and 3.00 ms latency.

7. **Knowledge Distillation for GCN-Based Neural Networks** - Bachelor Thesis, traffic volume prediction. Achieved 50% model compression on ASTGCN and improved the student model from 30% to 60% accuracy.

8. **Multimodal Image-Table Pipeline** - Multimodal document pipeline, paper in preparation. Built a graph-based splitting and alignment pipeline for images, tables, and text.
